## Course guidance and team decisions

Current official instructions and announcements determine course requirements. Use the rendered CS2103/T pages; raw website source can contain other courses, retired instructions, and future drafts. Semester-specific exceptions take precedence over older generic text.

The [Developer Guide's product scope](../docs/DeveloperGuide.md#product-scope) records StudentBook's MVP and feature owners. The MVP covers schooling level, subjects, guardian contact, weekly lesson slots, and hourly rate. Coordinate work with the relevant owner and confirm unresolved behavior with the team. Course examples do not add features to our scope or settle product decisions.

These guides are a dated planning reference. They do not certify that earlier tasks have been completed. Check current issues, PRs, reviews, merges, release assets, published pages, dashboards, and submission confirmations before marking an obligation complete. Keep confidential evaluation responses and submission credentials out of public planning records.

## Keeping the guides current

Update the relevant guide when a course instruction changes or a tentative week becomes published. Include the official source, the date checked, and any changed deadline or required artifact. Follow the [refresh procedure](06-sources-and-refresh.md#refresh-procedure) and submit the update through a reviewed PR. Keep course obligations separate from decisions about what StudentBook will implement.
