# Sources, coverage, and refresh procedure

Checked **12 October 2026**, Singapore time. Use this index to trace the planning guides to official instructions and keep them current.

## Coverage

The research covered **all 20 entries in the TP navigation**, from Overview through Supervision/Guidance, plus **36 weekly pages**: the summary, project, admin, and tutorial tabs for Weeks 5–13. Nested guidance and FAQ answers were inspected through the live browser and the official website source, including repeated embedded panels, optional deliverable details, screenshot example tabs, and the PE response/triage instructions. The guide consolidates requirements and exceptions instead of storing a verbatim website mirror.

The three tentative future weeks are intentionally link-only. Some diagram-popup triggers were rendered as plain text rather than working controls; their official source captions and links were inspected. Canvas-only materials, private submissions, TEAMMATES forms/results, and linked videos are not represented as opened, watched, submitted, or verified. Their instructions and access points remain in the relevant weekly page. This reference cannot establish that the team completed a task.

The [machine-readable source index](source-index.json) lists all 56 core URLs, check date, captured title, source hash, tentative status, and the guide covering them. It contains no private form responses or copied page bodies. Source hashes identify what was fetched, not a guarantee that a live page has stayed unchanged. In the JSON, `page_tentative_banner` describes that individual page. Its `status` also accounts for the weekly summary, so a future TP page remains tentative even if it has no banner of its own.

## Refresh procedure

1. Establish today's Singapore date and the correct academic year/semester. Do not infer the week from an old branch name or an earlier planning note.
2. Open that week's summary and check its tentative banner. Then read the project, admin, and tutorial tabs, plus the detailed TP page. Expand the relevant nested panels and FAQs. Repeated content should be interpreted consistently, not treated as a second separate obligation.
3. Follow links for rules affecting the task: constraints, standards, deliverables, reuse, freeze, grading, or PE. Check current announcements and user-accessible Canvas instructions for changes and submission destinations.
4. Record each required action with owner (each student/team/one representative), deadline/timezone, exact required artifact/path/title, destination, and completion evidence. Keep recommended and optional items visibly distinct.
5. For a formerly tentative week, replace the link-only entry only after publication is confirmed. Do not carry forward draft dates, guess submission filenames, or assume a past year's PE process.
6. For a conflict, preserve both source links and explain which current instruction is more specific. A semester-specific waiver beats a generic checklist; a specific deadline beats the normal Friday checkpoint. Ask only if the live sources leave a material unresolved ambiguity.
7. Check the actual repository, GitHub, published website, or submission receipt before marking an obligation complete. A local file, draft PR, or green unit-test run is not proof of release, review, publication, or Canvas submission.
8. Update the affected planning guides and their check dates through a reviewed PR. When rechecking indexed pages, update their entries in `source-index.json`, including status and the retrieved HTML hash. Keep the scope and date of each check accurate; do not imply every source was refreshed when only some were.

## Important interpretation traps

- The 500–600 LoC estimate is a rough effort indicator, not a quota or automatic full marks.
- Coverage percentage and Codecov status are not graded targets, but automated-test evidence and escaped defects matter.
- The optional PPP and removed demo notices supersede the generic text still displayed nearby.
- A current product screenshot is distinct from the early intended-UI mockup.
- General permission for proposed DG implementations does not mean unimplemented behavior may be advertised as available in the UG.
- Weeks 11–13 remain tentative even if an individual linked admin page lacks a banner; the summary marks the week's material tentative.
- The published Week 10 freeze explanation can be recorded now, while its future application dates still require confirmation.
- PE bug types are not interchangeable labels for maximizing marks, and an incorrect label does not invalidate a real defect.
- The final assessment uses contribution evidence and judgment. Task counts, PR counts, green dashboards, and AI confidence are not substitutes for quality.
